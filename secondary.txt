TIER 2 SHELF, RANKED BY QUOTATIONS PROVABLY FOUND IN THE PRIMARY TEXTS
Made by bin/schop-secondary.js. Ranking is by measurement and by reading, never by reputation.

HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS
   tested = quoted passages of 40+ characters the study offers between quote marks.
   provable = how many quoted passages were found word for word in one of his own texts,
   over runs of 30 characters, with capital letters and the old long s made to agree.
   The figure is a FLOOR: two layers of OCR sit between a study and the primary text, and a
   The longer the run demanded, the fewer the matches: always name the length with the figure.
   CITE THE TOOL WITH THE NUMBER: the counts depend on how a quotation is cut out of the page,
   so bin/schop-audit.js and this script disagree a little about the same study (Volkelt: 46
   provable here, 34 there, both at 30 characters). Both are floors; neither is a fidelity score.
   A study written in ENGLISH that quotes in translation is invisible to this test: its low
   number means the method cannot see it, not that it misquotes. Such studies are listed apart,
   and are NOT ranked against the German ones.

WHAT THIS SHELF IS FOR
   A tier-2 item is worth what its references are worth. Where a study cites by volume and
   page (file 28) its apparatus can be tested and it is worth more to a reader who must prove
   a sentence; where a study reports talk and letters (files 72, 73) its value is presence, and
   quoting it as though it quoted the published works will mislead. Never carry a secondary
   sentence into an answer about doctrine without finding the primary place.
