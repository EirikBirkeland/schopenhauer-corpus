THE TITLE CORRECTS THIS CATALOGUE. Pass 1 recorded a missing piece as "Ueber das Interesse an der Philosophie"; the volume's own table of contents names no such piece - what it names at S. 129 is "Ueber die Universitaets-Philosophie", and that is what is here. This is the text behind every story of his contempt for the professors: the thinker as the abnormal creature whose instinctive, objective, quixotic devotion to a work not his own is exactly what outlives him, with Aristotle's Ethics quoted against those who advise human thoughts for humans. If a RAG answer on "his quarrel with the universities" rests on a biography, it rests on sand; this is the ground.
