# note: ADDED IN PASS 14, which finally opened the FIRST band of 1851 - the five pieces the collection had never held in German, the Aphorismen (file 04) being the exception. The ghosts essay, 27,131 words, the largest of the five and the one a collection will want when the question is what he made of spirits, ghosts, somnambulism and second sight. Its conclusion, as this copy gives it, is worth having in the register because gossip outruns it: he is willing to admit a real influence of the dead on the living as POSSIBLE, but then it could happen only most seldom and exceptionally, since it would depend on conditions rarely met together; and even then the "empoerend absurde, ja niedertraechtig dumme Weltordnung" that would follow from the spirits' own accounts gains no objectively real ground, but goes to the account of the seer's own mind - "aegri somnia". This is the text, and the poodle is the gossip (T3-09).
