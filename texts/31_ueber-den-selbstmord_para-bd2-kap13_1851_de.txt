THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MORE THAN THE WORDS: the German prints paragraph numbers (§ 157 to § 160) and Saunders's volumes carry almost none - file 15 has 7 section marks in 172,000 words, file 19 has none, file 18 has 7. So an English-only reader of Parerga cannot cite it by paragraph at all, and a RAG answer that quotes "§ 157" of Parerga has taken the number from a German edition whether it knows it or not.
