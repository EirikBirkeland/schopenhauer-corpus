IT OPENS WITH A CLAIM FOR THE CLAIM-SHELF, in its first line: "Wie die Lehre Schopenhauer's, so ist auch seine Person falsch aufgefasst und in ueblen Ruf gebracht worden" - as his doctrine, so his person, wrongly conceived and brought into a bad reputation: the programme of the whole piece, and an admission that by 1877 the popular image was already fixed. WHAT IT SUPPLIES, read and set down: the Berlin habilitation in 1820 and the statement that he doctored there for ONE SEMESTER ONLY, "die Zeit, in der ein Hegel und Schleiermacher an der berliner Universitaet dominirten", being the reason given (see G-37); the two passages that are the best evidence in the collection for the dog as companion - on his walks he was mostly alone, "nur von seinem Pudel begleitet", and in his room "neben dem Sofa ruhte sein Pudel auf einem schwarzen Baerenfelle", with the dog-pictures of Woollet and Ridinger round him - yet still NOT ONE word of the dogs' names, so Atma and Butz remain outside every witness this collection holds (G-03); and, from the will, that his universal heir was a Berlin fund for those who upheld and restored lawful order in the uprisings of 1848 and 1849. WHAT IT DOES NOT SUPPLY, after a folded search for zwei Uhr, 2 Uhr, zwei Stunden, Uhr, Vorlesungen, dozirte, docirte, Semester, Privatdocent: the HOUR of the lectures. Not here, not in Gwinner 1862, not in Gwinner 1863, not in Schemann's letters - the two o'clock that was set to starve Hegel is not in any of these four witnesses (G-37).
