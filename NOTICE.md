# NOTICE

## What this is

A tiered corpus of Arthur Schopenhauer (1788-1860) assembled for retrieval: his own
printed texts, studies about him, and a shelf of claims about him. Items are recorded
with edition, translator, source URL and date read, and are tiered - not ordered by
reputation.

## Copyright

- **The words are public domain.** He died in 1860; the English here was published in
  1883-1907 and is in the public domain in the United States and, generally, elsewhere.
- **The transcriptions are somebody else's work.** German originals were taken from
  `zeno.org` (transcriptions of the *Zürcher Ausgabe*, a scholarly reading edition with
  notes, not a full critical apparatus), from `de.wikisource.org`, and from scans on
  `archive.org` (F. A. Brockhaus; Julius Frauenstädt's collected edition). Those scans
  carry the usual OCR damage, quoted from in this corpus only where read line by line.

  General Public License, version 3 or later, see `LICENSE`.

## Two warnings before quoting

1. **Coordinates belong to a printing.** A `§`, chapter or volume number is an address
   only once the printing is named: the 1877 collected *Parerga* numbers its sections one
   ahead of the 1851 first edition, and English volumes re-cut and re-order German ones.
   Page markers in square brackets were recovered from running heads and are editorial,
   file by file; `0` in the index means "cite by section, not page".
2. **Two absences are recorded as absences.** `NO DATA` = searched and not found;
   `UNVERIFIED` = one source only. `gaps.txt` states what the collection still lacks -
   including author's footnotes missing from two German transcriptions (`GAP-9`).
